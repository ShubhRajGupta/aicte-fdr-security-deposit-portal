# Style profile learned from `60427.pdf`

## Scope
This profile comes from the user's six-page SIH 2025 idea-presentation PDF. Treat it as a reference for density, organization and diagram use. It is not an editable template and not evidence that its technical claims should be reused.

## Reusable visual patterns by page
- **Page 1 — Title:** SIH heading/logo at the top; title-page heading; required PS/team metadata arranged as a readable information block; substantial project-related visual on the opposite side. Preserve the official template's placement and use the available body area efficiently.
- **Page 2 — Proposed solution:** large central heading; clear proposed-solution section title; a compact solution-name/full-expansion line; short description and numbered/short highlight list on one side; a labeled technical illustration on the other.
- **Page 3 — Technical approach:** dense technical labels and grouped method steps on one side; a system architecture diagram on the other. Show process direction, modules, metrics and feedback paths with explicit labels.
- **Page 4 — Feasibility and viability:** two-column organization; framework/compatibility and feasibility on one side; criteria/algorithmic logic and outcome indicators on the other. Use concise risk/mitigation pairings where supported.
- **Page 5 — Impact and benefits:** grouped short sections such as research impact, technical impact, practical benefits and broader outcomes. Avoid a single undifferentiated bullet wall; separate sections visibly.
- **Page 6 — Research and references:** two-column reference groupings; primary source, supporting works, frameworks, tools and evaluation environment. Make references compact but readable and verifiable.

## What to improve from the sample
- The team mark splits `Contrast` awkwardly as `Contra / st`. Never split a word. For `Phra Uma Thewi`, use intentional line breaks only at word boundaries and fit the existing fixed mark.
- Avoid long, full-sentence paragraphs. Use compact technical fragments, precise labels and short numbered points.
- The sample has long bullet lists; keep the density but make it scan-friendly using partitions, compact tables and diagrams.
- Metrics printed in the sample (for example, path/crash/state percentages on pages 4–5) are project-specific claims. Do not copy them into any new SIH deck without independent verification for the current project.
- References are concentrated on page 6 in the sample; the user's new rule requires numeric source markers on every slide plus a complete bibliography on page 6.
- Keep blue footer, SIH branding, logos, title locations and master-layout elements unchanged when the actual template is supplied.

## Style principles
- Arial across editable text
- clear H1/H2/body/caption hierarchy
- short exact phrases, no emojis
- numbered points and stages where they improve navigation
- compulsory internal content partitions on every slide
- compact tables/diagrams and relevant images
- selective bold, underline, text accent and highlighting
- references on every slide
- dense but legible; no minimalist emptiness and no overcrowded text walls
- exact team name: `Phra Uma Thewi`
- never carry over the sample team's identity or problem-specific claims
